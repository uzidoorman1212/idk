Study map

Five ways to understand Viking society

Each section connects a social-studies idea to the people, places, and choices behind Viking life.

01
⌖

Geography & origins

Scandinavia, surrounding seas, and the long routes that connected faraway communities.

Vikings came from Scandinavia, a region of forests, fjords, and islands in northern Europe. Their ships could travel through the Baltic Sea, North Sea, Atlantic Ocean, and Mediterranean routes.

02
↔

Trade & exchange

How goods, ideas, and people moved across Europe and the wider world.

Viking traders exchanged furs, slaves, timber, iron, wool, and luxury goods. Their networks linked Scandinavia with Britain, Ireland, Russia, Byzantium, and North America.

03
✦

Religion & belief

Norse mythology, spiritual rituals, and the importance of the gods in daily life.

Vikings worshipped gods such as Odin, Thor, and Freyja. Runestones, burial goods, and place names reveal how religion shaped memory and community identity.

04
◉

Community & power

Families, farms, crafts, leaders, and the social roles that shaped Viking life.

Viking communities included farmers, craftspeople, sailors, traders, and warriors. Leaders gained influence through wealth, military skill, and relationships across communities.

05
⌁

Evidence & sources

How archaeological finds and historical records help us work with evidence.

Archaeologists study coins, weapons, tools, textiles, and burial sites. Because written sources were written later, historians compare multiple sources before drawing conclusions.
